Scam Guide

Common Online Scams

Scammers usually rely on tricking people rather than breaking computers. Explore the five main types of scams below to see how they work and how to avoid them.

Fake Links & Messages · High Risk

1. Phishing Scams

Scammers send fake text messages or emails that look like your bank, electricity board, or courier company. They include a link to a fake website that steals your username, password, or card details.

  • Messages claiming your bank account or SIM is blocked
  • Fake website addresses with tiny spelling differences
  • Requests to enter your secret passwords and PINs

Secret Code Theft · High Risk

2. OTP Fraud

Scammers call you pretending to be bank executives, electricity officers, or lottery managers. They try to convince you to read out your 6-digit OTP code or ask you to install screen-sharing apps.

  • Urgent phone calls warning you about fake charges
  • Asking you to share the SMS verification code
  • Asking you to download apps like AnyDesk or TeamViewer
Read Detailed Guide →
Payment & QR Traps · High Risk

3. UPI Scams

Scammers trick people who are not familiar with how UPI works. They send QR codes or payment requests claiming you need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money or get a refund.

  • Remember: You never enter a UPI PIN to receive money
  • Scanning a QR code sends money out of your account
  • Fake buyers on marketplace websites like OLX

Work From Home Traps · Medium Risk

4. Fake Job Scams

Unsolicited messages on WhatsApp or Telegram offering huge daily salaries for easy tasks like liking videos or giving 5-star ratings. Once you join, they ask you to deposit money to unlock your earnings.

  • Offers of ₹3,000–₹5,000 per day with no experience
  • Small initial payments given to gain your trust
  • Demands to pay "security deposits" or "VIP fees"
Read Detailed Guide →
Fake Shopping Websites · Medium Risk

5. Online Shopping Scams

Fake online stores advertised on Instagram or Facebook offering luxury items at impossible 90% discounts. They take advance payment through UPI or card, but the product never arrives.

  • Unbelievable discounts like smartphones for ₹1,299
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) is disabled at checkout
  • No real contact phone number, address, or return policy
