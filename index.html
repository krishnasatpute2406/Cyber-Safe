Simple Safety Basics

Why Online Safety Matters

Most online scams today do not involve complicated computer hacking. Instead, scammers trick people by creating panic, pretending to be from a bank, or promising easy money.

01. PROTECT YOUR MONEY

Avoid Financial Loss

Scammers send fake alerts about disconnected electricity or blocked accounts to rush you into making mistakes. Remembering simple rules keeps your money safe in your bank account.

02. PROTECT YOUR IDENTITY

Keep Details Private

Your phone number, passwords, PAN card, and Aadhaar card belong only to you. Keeping them private stops scammers from taking fake loans or opening accounts in your name.

03. USE THE INTERNET SAFELY

Browse with Confidence

When you know how to check website addresses and recognize fake calls, you can send payments, shop online, and use digital services without fear.

Common Threats

5 Common Scams You Should Know

Click any topic below to learn how scammers operate, what warning signs to watch for, and how to protect yourself.

Fake Links & Messages · High Risk

1. Phishing Scams

Fake emails, text messages, or websites that copy official banks or companies to steal your login passwords and card numbers.

  • Messages claiming your account is blocked
  • Fake website links with slight spelling mistakes
  • Requests to enter your secret passwords
Learn About Phishing →
Secret Codes · High Risk

2. OTP Fraud

Callers pretending to be bank managers who ask for your secret One-Time Password (OTP) or ask you to install screen-sharing apps.

  • Callers claiming an emergency withdrawal happened
  • Asking you to read out the 6-digit SMS code
  • Asking you to install apps like AnyDesk or TeamViewer
Learn About OTP Fraud →
Digital Payments · High Risk

3. UPI Scams

Tricks where scammers ask you to scan a QR code or enter your UPI PIN promising you will receive money or cashback.

  • Golden Rule: UPI PIN is only needed to SEND money
  • Scanning a QR code sends money OUT of your account
  • Fake buyers on OLX sending payment requests
Learn About UPI Scams →
Work from Home Traps · Medium Risk

4. Fake Job Scams

Messages on WhatsApp or Telegram offering big daily earnings for easy tasks like liking YouTube videos, then asking you to pay money to unlock earnings.

  • Promises of ₹3,000–₹5,000 per day for very simple tasks
  • Small initial payments given to win your trust
  • Asking you to pay deposits for "VIP tasks"
Learn About Fake Jobs →
Fake Shopping Websites · Medium Risk

5. Online Shopping Scams

Fake online stores on social media offering 90% discounts on expensive items. They take advance payment and never deliver the item.

  • Brand new luxury phones or shoes for just ₹999
  • No Cash on Delivery (COD) option available
  • No real contact phone number or office address
Learn About Shopping Scams →
Immediate Help

Victim of an Online Scam? Dial 1930

If money was taken from your bank account or UPI without your permission, dial 1930 immediately. Reporting quickly gives police and banks the chance to freeze the stolen funds before the scammer can withdraw them.
